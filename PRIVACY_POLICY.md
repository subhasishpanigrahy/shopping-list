# Privacy Policy for Shopping List

**Last Updated:** October 31, 2026

Welcome to **Shopping List** ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect (Zero Remote Data Storage)
Shopping List operates on a **Local-First Privacy Architecture**:
- **Offline & On-Device**: Your shopping checklist items, quantities, categories, custom stores, and notes are stored strictly in your device's local memory (`SharedPreferences` / SQLite).
- **No Account Required**: You do not need to register, provide an email address, or log in to use Shopping List.
- **No Cloud Synchronization of Checklists**: We do not transmit, sync, or sell your shopping items or personal shopping habits to external servers.

---

## 2. External Retailers & Affiliate Disclosure
Shopping List is a universal shopping organizer and aggregator that connects users to third-party retail destinations (such as Amazon, Flipkart, Blinkit, Myntra, Ajio, Nykaa, and others).

### Affiliate Link Transparency
- When you tap on a store card or external link to view products or make purchases, you may be redirected to third-party retail platforms or their native mobile applications.
- Shopping List participates in affiliate marketing programs (including Amazon Associates, Flipkart Affiliate, and affiliate aggregator networks such as Cuelinks).
- If you click on an external link and complete a qualifying purchase within the retailer's session window, **we may earn a small referral commission at absolutely zero additional cost to you**.
- These commissions support ongoing app development, maintenance, and server uptime.

---

## 3. Third-Party Web Sites and Apps
When you tap an external retailer link, you are subject to that retailer's independent terms of service, cookies, and privacy policies. We do not control and are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party retail services.

---

## 4. App Permissions
Shopping List requests only the minimum Android permissions necessary for core operation:
- `INTERNET`: Required to query live store web search landing pages and open store links via the in-app browser.
- `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`: Used to verify internet connectivity before loading store web pages.

We do **not** request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photo gallery, or device location.

---

## 5. Children's Privacy
Shopping List does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13.

---

## 6. Updates to this Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available through the app.
